Chapter Two

The Kingdom at the Edge of the World

✦ ✦ ✦

From Seattle, the Charmings crossed the border into the great northern kingdom of Canada, bound for the shining city of Vancouver. The journey itself was something of a wonder — for before the adventures on the ground could begin, a great simulated flight over the Canadian Rockies awaited.

A kingdom of ice and stone, stretching to the horizon

Prince and Snow were swept up into the sky, and mountain after mountain rolled past beneath them, vast and ancient and indifferent to the small humans gaping at their magnificence. Snow pressed her nose to the glass. The Prince pretended to be calm about the whole affair and largely succeeded.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Bridge at the Top of the World

Next came the bridge. Not merely any bridge — a great suspension bridge, strung impossibly high above the forest floor, swaying gently in the mountain air as if to remind all who crossed it that the ground was very, very far below. Snow crossed with the confidence of someone who had, after all, recently stood upside down in a museum. The Prince crossed with the confidence of someone who was definitely not looking down.

They made it across. Both of them. This was considered a triumph.

High above the world, where the brave dare to tread
✦ ✦ ✦

All Aboard

And so, with mountains conquered and bridges crossed, the Charmings at last made their way to the harbor, where their home for the next week sat waiting: the magnificent Nieuw Amsterdam. She was grand and white and enormous, and she pointed her bow northward toward Alaska as if she already knew where she was going and was simply waiting for the Charmings to catch up.

Prince and Snow climbed aboard, found their small but cozy cabin high up on the ship with its wonderful views, and settled in. The adventure was no longer coming. It had begun.

The great ship awaited — their home for the week ahead
