Chapter Five

Juneau & the Glacier

The following day was the biggest day of the cruise — indeed, it may have been the biggest day of the entire adventure. The Charmings had arrived in Juneau, and Snow was celebrating the anniversary of her birth. Prince had been planning a most extraordinary gift, and the day was about to begin.

Juneau is a peculiar sort of city, reachable only by sea or by air, nestled between glacier-carved mountains and the cold Alaskan waters. It is the kind of place that reminds you how very large and untamed the world truly is — a fitting backdrop for what was to come.

The Magic Carpet to the Ice

Prince had arranged for a magic carpet of a modern kind — a helicopter — to carry Snow up and over those great mountains and set her down upon a living glacier. Not many people can say they have walked upon ancient ice, upon a river of frozen time that has been slowly moving, slowly carving, since before any kingdom existed. Snow Charming was about to become one of them.

Views from Above

As the helicopter rose above the city and banked toward the mountains, the world below shrank to something small and manageable. The jagged peaks, the dark forests, the glittering water of the inlet — all of it spread out like a map, like a kingdom seen from the clouds. The Charmings pressed their faces to the glass and could not look away.

Upon the Glacier

And then they were there. On the ice. The helicopter settled with a gentle crunch of runners on snow, the rotors slowed, and the door opened onto a world of impossible blue and white silence. Snow Charming stepped out and stood upon a glacier that has been forming and flowing for thousands of years, a river of ice moving so slowly it measures its journey in inches per year rather than miles per hour.

"Hard to describe the glowing blue of the glacial ice — blue only beneath the surface, where light travels through ten thousand years of compression and emerges changed."

And they captured it all — including a moment of the living glacier itself, the ice and the sky and the silence that is not quite silence:

A Royal Birthday Dinner

Once the magic carpet ride was over and the Charmings had returned to the warmth of the Nieuw Amsterdam, Prince brought Snow to a dinner worthy of a birthday celebration. Filet mignon. Ribeye. The kind of meal that marks a day as truly special, shared between two people who have been on adventures together for twenty-five years and would not trade a single one.

This was the birthday of Snow's dreams, and it went very well indeed.
