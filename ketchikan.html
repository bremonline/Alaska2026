Chapter Three

The Fishing Village at the Edge of the Rain

✦ ✦ ✦

The first day aboard the Nieuw Amsterdam was a day of pure sea — no land in sight, only waves and sky and the distant suggestion of adventure ahead. The Charmings explored the ship, discovered the piano bars and music lounges, and attended the World Stage, where the entertainments were grand and the seats were comfortable. It was, by any measure, a very fine day for doing nothing in particular.

But on the second day, land appeared. The great ship slowed, and the fishing village of Ketchikan emerged from the mist — colorful and small and smelling magnificently of the sea.

✦ ✦ ✦

The Village in the Rain

The village at the edge of the world

Ketchikan is the kind of place that takes its rain seriously. It is, by reputation, one of the wettest towns in all of North America, and it wears this distinction with a certain cheerful pride. The Charmings walked the river path along Ketchikan Creek, keeping a hopeful eye out for bears. The salmon were everywhere — leaping and thrashing and, in many cases, simply lying there in that particular state that salmon reach at the end of their long journey.

The bears, however, were not cooperating. Rumor had it that later in the day one was spotted, and a toddler had to be briefly rescued from its grasp. The Charmings missed all of this entirely, which was probably for the best.

It should be noted that Ketchikan did not, on this particular day, live up to its reputation. The skies were light and cloudy, the temperature a delightful 70 degrees, and not a single drop of rain fell upon the Charmings. The village, apparently, had decided to put its best foot forward.

Salmon by the thousands, bears by none

Instead, they took to the skies. Flyover Ketchikan offered a bird's eye view of the village, the waterways, and the vast green wilderness stretching in every direction — the kind of perspective that makes a person feel both very small and very fortunate at the same time. Snow was delighted. The Prince was also delighted, and slightly less nervous than he had been over the Rockies.

Where the rainforest meets the sea
✦ ✦ ✦

Home on the Water

At the end of the day, the Charmings returned to their floating kingdom. The Nieuw Amsterdam gleamed in the harbor, patient and grand, waiting to carry them onward to whatever came next. Prince and Snow stood together on the dock, looked up at the great white ship, and felt the particular contentment of travelers who know that the best is still ahead.

Their home for the week — the magnificent Nieuw Amsterdam
