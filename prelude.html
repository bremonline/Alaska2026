Once Upon a Time

✦ ✦ ✦

As we all know, the story itself ended with Prince and Snow Charming living happily ever after. But that was only the beginning. Prince and Snow's adventures were not at an end — rather, they were just beginning. There was a story of Life After Happily Ever After, where Prince and Snow had many adventures. They passed through the Panama Canal on the Amsterdam, Snow had an accident and broke her leg, they went on their first visit to a far away land called England, and they had two lovely princesses. But that story, as exciting as it was, is not this story.

Years have passed since that story. The Princesses grew up. One has finished her apprenticeship and is now imparting her wisdom on others, and the other is completing her apprenticeship soon. We could talk all about their adventures, and how they came to be the wonderful women they are — but that is also not this story.

This story is about a single adventure that Prince and Snow Charming completed to celebrate 25 years of their union. They decided to embark on an adventure to an exciting land that we shall call Alaska. Alaska on their anniversary is cold and harsh and unforgiving, but the Charmings were clever and picked a time when it was more hospitable — so they delayed the start until the weather turned better.
